---

## Course Objectives

The course aims to:

1. Provide understanding of fundamental concepts of linear algebra and their applications.  
2. Develop mathematical models using linear algebra for problems across disciplines.  
3. Enable formulation of problems in science and engineering, including data analytics and deep learning.  

---

## Course Outcomes

On completion, students will be able to:

- **CO1:** Model situations using vectors, matrices, and linear equations.  
- **CO2:** Apply mathematical concepts in computer and allied sciences.  
- **CO3:** Understand eigenvalues and eigenvectors in applications like graphics.  
- **CO4:** Apply SVD in machine learning, graphics, compression, and image processing.

## Syllabus

### Unit I: Introduction to Linear Algebra (1 Hour)

- Linearity vs non-linearity  
- Real-life examples  
- Overview of linear algebra  
- Applications: Google PageRank, Google Maps  

---

### Unit II: System of Linear Equations (8 Hours)

- Vectors and linear combinations  
- Visualization of equations  
- Matrix elimination  
- Rank of matrix  
- Echelon & normal forms  
- Homogeneous & non-homogeneous systems  
- Applications:
  - Machine learning  
  - Cryptography  
  - Color models  

---

### Unit III: Vector Spaces & Linear Transformation (12 Hours)

- Vector spaces and subspaces  
- Linear span  
- Independence/dependence  
- Basis and dimension  
- Linear mappings  
- Kernel, image, null space  
- Rank-nullity theorem  
- Singular & non-singular mappings  
- Isomorphisms  
- Similarity of matrices  
- Change of basis  
- Inner product spaces  
- Norms  
- Orthogonality  
- Gram-Schmidt process  
- Applications:
  - Data smoothing  
  - Image scaling  

---

### Unit IV: Eigenvalues & Eigenvectors (8 Hours)

- Characteristic polynomial  
- Cayley-Hamilton theorem  
- Eigenvalues and eigenvectors  
- Geometric interpretation  
- Diagonalization  
- Powers and functions of matrices  
- Symmetric matrices  
- Quadratic forms  
- Least squares method  
- Applications:
  - Facial recognition  
  - Feature extraction  
  - Search engines  

---

### Unit V: Singular Value Decomposition (8 Hours)

- Spectral decomposition  
- SVD  
- Rank-k approximation  
- Power method  
- Applications:
  - PCA  
  - Data centering  
  - Document ranking  
  - Clustering  

---

### Unit VI: Applications of Linear Algebra (8 Hours)

**Computational Approach:**

- Traffic systems  
- Circuit analysis  
- Chemical equation balancing  
- Data compression (SVD)  
- Word embeddings  
- Bias in data  
- Markov matrices (PageRank)  
- Game strategies  
- Information retrieval  
- Chessboard moves  
- Genetics modeling
